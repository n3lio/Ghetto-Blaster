Welcome

Point Ghetto Blaster at a folder containing your music. We'll scan it once and you're ready to play.

Supported: MP3, M4A, FLAC, OGG, WAV, AAC. You can add more folders later in Settings.

Connected

You're paired with the desktop player on your network. From here you can browse the library, control playback, and queue tracks — the audio plays on the desktop.
